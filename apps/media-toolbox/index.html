Media Toolbox

Classroom media tools that run entirely in your browser — no uploads, nothing to install. Pick a tool to get started.

🔄
Video RotateAvailable
Fix sideways or upside-down videos. Rotation flag only — no re-encoding, zero quality loss, instant even for large files.
🔇
Remove AudioAvailable
Strip the audio track to make a silent video. The video is untouched and it finishes instantly (audio bytes remain in the file but are unused).
🎵
Extract AudioAvailable
Pull the audio out of a video losslessly as .m4a, or convert it to a universal WAV.
📦
Compress VideoAvailable
Shrink a video to a target size (e.g. 10/25 MB) for email or chat. Runs fully in your browser.
🫥
Video MosaicNew
Draw a box over a face, plate or logo — mosaic, blur or cover it. Optional simple motion tracking (beta).
